# Choosing Your Church CRM: RockRMS vs Planning Center vs GoHighLevel

Most churches are using outdated databases that don't talk to each other. In this post, we break down the three most popular CRM options for church follow-up pipelines.

## 1. Planning Center (PCO)
The most popular church software for a reason. Its strength is its ecosystem.
- **Pros:** Great interface, volunteer-friendly, massive integration with Mailchimp/Zapier.
- **Cons:** Can get expensive as you add more modules (People, Services, Check-ins).
- **Best for:** Small to mid-sized churches that want a clean, "it just works" experience.

## 2. RockRMS
The "Gold Standard" for mid-to-large churches. It’s open-source, highly customizable, and incredibly powerful.
- **Pros:** Native attendance-aware automation, deep data tracking, free (if you self-host).
- **Cons:** Steep learning curve, requires technical expertise or a partner like HappyWP to set up.
- **Best for:** Mid-to-large churches that want a "forever" system that can scale to any complexity.

## 3. GoHighLevel (GHL)
Not a "church" software, but a marketing-style automation CRM.
- **Pros:** Best-in-class SMS/Email automation, visual drag-and-drop builder, landing page creator.
- **Cons:** Not attendance-aware (no check-in system), can feel too "corporate" for some churches.
- **Best for:** Churches that prioritize lead magnet conversion and high-volume communication above all else.

## Which One is Right for You?
The best CRM is the one your team will actually use. If you have a highly technical volunteer, RockRMS is a no-brainer. If you want simplicity and clean UI, Planning Center is the winner. If you're building a massive digital reach program, GoHighLevel might be the way to go.

### Recommendation
For most churches, Nathan recommends a "RockRMS + Custom Workflows" setup. It gives you the best of both worlds: native church data and the power of full automation.
