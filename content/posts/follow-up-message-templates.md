# 5 Church Follow-Up Message Templates You Can Copy-Paste Today

Most church follow-up emails are too long, too generic, and ask for too much. In this post, we provide the exact templates Nathan recommends for your first-time and second-time visitor pipelines.

## 1. The Instant Welcome (SMS)
**When:** Within 2 hours of visit.
**Goal:** Acknowledge and thank.

> "Hi [Name]! This is Pastor [Name] from [Church]. We’re so glad you joined us today. If there’s any way we can pray for you, just reply to this text. Hope you have a great Sunday!"

## 2. The Personal Follow-Up (Email)
**When:** Monday morning (or 24 hours after visit).
**Goal:** Human touch and personal connection.

> Subject: So glad you visited [Church]!
>
> "Hi [Name],
> 
> My name is [Name] and I’m on staff here at [Church]. I just wanted to personally thank you for visiting yesterday. I hope you felt welcomed and enjoyed the service.
> 
> If you have any questions about the church or if you’d like to grab a quick coffee, I’d love to connect. Have a great week!
> 
> Best,
> [Name]"

## 3. The Second-Time Visitor (SMS)
**When:** 48 hours after their second visit.
**Goal:** Invite to connection event.

> "Hi [Name]! It was great to see you back at [Church] this past Sunday! We’re hosting a quick 'Connect Lunch' after service next week—would love for you to join us. Can I save you a seat?"

## 4. The Salvation/Baptism Decision (Email)
**When:** Same day as decision.
**Goal:** Immediate discipleship handoff.

> Subject: The best decision you've ever made!
>
> "Hi [Name],
> 
> We are so excited about the decision you made today! This is the start of an incredible journey with Jesus. 
> 
> I’ve CC’d [Name] on this email—they help lead our discipleship team and would love to send you a quick guide on 'What's Next' and talk about baptism.
> 
> Celebrating with you!
> [Name]"

## 5. The Serving/Small Group Signup (Email)
**When:** 24 hours after signup.
**Goal:** Leader intro.

> Subject: Welcome to the [Ministry/Group] team!
>
> "Hi [Name],
> 
> We saw you signed up for [Group/Ministry]—that's awesome! [Leader Name] is the leader of that area and will reach out to you within the next 48 hours with details on your first meeting.
> 
> Thanks for stepping up to serve/connect!
> [Name]"

## Key Principle: Keep it Short
Notice that none of these are longer than 4-5 sentences. The goal is connection, not a newsletter. Keep it personal, keep it brief, and always include a single clear next step.
