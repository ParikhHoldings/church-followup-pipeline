# The 24-Hour Rule: Why Church Follow-Up Timing is Everything

When it comes to church visitors, the clock is ticking from the moment they pull out of your parking lot. In this post, we explore why timing is the single most important factor in whether a visitor becomes a member.

## The Shrinking Window of Connection
Years of data from church growth consultants show a clear trend: the "half-life" of a visitor's interest is incredibly short. 

- **Contacted in 24 hours:** 85% return rate.
- **Contacted in 48-72 hours:** 60% return rate.
- **Contacted in 7+ days:** Less than 15% return rate.

If your process involves "processing cards on Tuesday" and "mailing a letter on Thursday," you have already lost the majority of your potential connections.

## Why Speed Matters
1. **Momentum:** Emotional momentum peaks during the service and fades rapidly.
2. **Value:** Quick follow-up signals that you actually value the person, not just the "headcount."
3. **Relevance:** By the time Thursday rolls around, the visitor has moved on to the stresses of the week.

## How to Automate the Speed
You don't need a staff member sitting by the computer 24/7. With modern CRMs like RockRMS or Planning Center, you can trigger an instant SMS and email the moment a digital connection card is submitted.

### The Perfect Timeline
- **Instant (0-2 hours):** SMS/Email "Thanks for coming!"
- **24 Hours:** Personal phone call from a pastor or team leader.
- **48 Hours:** Invite to a specific next step (Small group, lunch, etc.).

Stop waiting until Tuesday. Start automating your welcome today.
