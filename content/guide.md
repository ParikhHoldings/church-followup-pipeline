# The Church Follow-Up Pipeline: A Complete Automation Blueprint

## Introduction: Why Most Churches Fail at Follow-Up
Most churches do a great job with the "first touch." You have a welcome team, you have coffee, and you have a friendly greeting. But the moment the service ends, the connection often breaks.

Data suggests that visitors who are contacted within 24 hours are significantly more likely to return than those contacted even 7 days later. Yet, most churches process connection cards on Tuesday or Wednesday, meaning the first real reach-out happens 3-4 days after the visit.

This guide is designed to close that gap. We’re moving from a manual, "when-we-have-time" model to an automated, high-touch pipeline that ensures no one falls through the cracks.

---

## 1. The 5 Critical Follow-Up Moments
Your church doesn't need a single follow-up process; it needs five distinct pipelines triggered by specific actions.

### 1.1 The First-Time Visitor (The Welcome Pipeline)
This is the most critical 72-hour window. The goal is to make them feel seen and valued without being overwhelmed.
- **Trigger:** Connection card submission (digital or physical entered into CRM).
- **Goal:** Acknowledge, thank, and provide one clear next step.

### 1.2 The Second-Time Visitor (The Return Pipeline)
When someone returns for a second week, they are no longer "just visiting." They are investigating. This requires a different tone.
- **Trigger:** Attendance check-in for the second time within 4 weeks.
- **Goal:** Invite them into a specific community or connection event (e.g., "Lunch with the Pastors").

### 1.3 Salvation or Baptism Decision (The Discipleship Pipeline)
When someone makes a life-changing decision, momentum is everything. A delay in follow-up here is a failure in discipleship.
- **Trigger:** Decision card or digital hand-raise.
- **Goal:** Immediate pastoral contact and connection to a mentor or baptism class.

### 1.4 Small Group or Ministry Signup (The Integration Pipeline)
People stay in churches because of relationships, not just services.
- **Trigger:** Sign-up form submission.
- **Goal:** Introduction to the leader and a 30-day "how is it going?" check-in.

### 1.5 Membership Class Completion (The Commitment Pipeline)
Membership isn't the finish line; it's the starting block.
- **Trigger:** Completion of the final membership class session.
- **Goal:** Serving-path placement and a six-month "integration audit."

## 2. The Anatomy of a Perfect Pipeline
Every moment above follows a structured sequence:

1. **The Instant Ack (0-2 Hours):**
   - **Channel:** SMS + Email.
   - **Message:** "Thanks for joining us today! We're so glad you were here. Here's a link to [resource/next step]."

2. **The Personal Touch (24 Hours):**
   - **Channel:** Phone Call or Personal Email.
   - **Owner:** Staff or Lead Volunteer.
   - **Script:** "Hi [Name], this is [Name] from [Church]. I just wanted to personally thank you for visiting. If you have any questions or if there's any way we can pray for you, let me know."

3. **The 'One Thing' (48-72 Hours):**
   - **Channel:** Email.
   - **Message:** "If you're looking for one way to get involved, we'd love for you to join us at [Specific Event] on [Date]. Hope to see you there!"

4. **The Handwritten Note (Day 4-5):**
   - **Channel:** Physical Mail.
   - **Owner:** Volunteer Team.
   - **Message:** A simple "So glad you were with us!" card.

## 4. The 90-Day Implementation Roadmap

### Phase 1: The Audit (Weeks 1-2)
Look at your data. How many visitors did you have last month? How many were contacted? Who did the contacting? Document the "leaks."

### Phase 2: The First-Time Visitor Pilot (Weeks 3-4)
Build one pipeline. Just one. Automate the first text and the first email. Train your team to make the 24-hour call.

### Phase 3: Scaling the Pipelines (Weeks 5-8)
Build the Second-Time, Decision, and Integration pipelines. Move your volunteer teams into their new ownership roles.

### Phase 4: Monitor and Optimize (Weeks 9-12)
Track your "Connection Rate." If it's not moving, adjust your message templates or your timing.

---

## 5. Metrics that Matter
Stop tracking just attendance. Start tracking:
- **Contact Rate:** Percentage of visitors contacted within 24 hours.
- **Return Rate:** Percentage of first-time visitors who return within 4 weeks.
- **Group Placement Rate:** Percentage of members in a small group.
